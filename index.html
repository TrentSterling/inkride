Connection & privacy details

Same-origin browser tabs use a direct BroadcastChannel link, including offline. Different browsers/devices use WebRTC; public MQTT brokers carry only discovery and encrypted signaling. The public playground is open to everyone using this build. For a private room, only share the generated code with your friends; anyone with the code can join. No accounts, analytics, or cloud saves. Props and track edits are shared; named sketchbook saves and restart bookmarks stay on your browser. Some networks block direct connections; this prototype does not include a paid TURN relay. A visible peer takes over hosting when the current host tab goes into the background. Browser suspension can still interrupt a session. Host migration resumes the most recent received state, not a deterministic rollback.

For reliable multi-tab play, open two copies of the same hosted URL (HTTPS, or localhost). A chat preview or file:// URL may have an isolated browser origin. The status above reports actual peer links; a discovery connection alone is not a connected friend.

Boost pushes in the direction you draw. Bounce throws you away from the line. Scenery never collides. Cut removes just the brushed section. Shift+Cut removes a whole stroke. Select a track to move it, rotate it, change its material, or bend it with Handles. Shift-click selects several items. All edits can be undone.

Drawing temporarily holds the camera still. Release the pen to resume following. A crash does not reset the track or anyone else. The last remaining rider keeps the world.

About · More by Tront

INKRIDE by Tront

INKRIDE is a free shared sketchbook with physics, in the spirit of Line Rider. Draw a track in ink, press Space, and a rider on a sled follows your line: boost ink for speed, bounce ink for lift, ramps, bowls and loops as presets, and physical toys to knock around. Bad landings are the fun part. Make something worth crashing.

Open a room and everyone in it draws on the same sheet and rides it together. Rooms find each other through public MQTT brokers and talk over WebRTC, tabs in one browser sync over BroadcastChannel, and nothing runs on a server of ours. Sketches and restart bookmarks save in your browser and export as files. One HTML file, Canvas 2D, no external assets, made by Tront (Trent Sterling).
